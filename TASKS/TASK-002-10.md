## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Mailable created at `App\Mail\RankingAlertEmail`

- [ ] Email subject: "[SEOIndicator] Alert: {keyword} {change_type}"
- [ ] Blade template at `resources/views/emails/ranking-alert.blade.php`
- [ ] Email body includes:

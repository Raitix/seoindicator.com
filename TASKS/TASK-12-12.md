### [2026-02-24 23:18] Agent: PreTester
**Root Cause Identified:** The `.htaccess` routing is incorrectly stripping `/api/` prefix before passing to Laravel.

**Analysis:**
- Current rule: `RewriteRule ^api/(.*)$ backend/public/index.php [L,QSA]`
- This captures everything AFTER `/api/` and passes to Laravel
- Laravel's `api.php` routes already expect `/api/` prefix (configured in `bootstrap/app.php`)
- Result: Request to `/api/health` becomes `/health` → 404

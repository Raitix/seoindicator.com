| CS-002-05 | Score by relevance | Multiple matches | Higher score first |

## Notes for Developer

1. **Service Location**: `backend/app/Services/CompetitorSuggester.php`

2. **API Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/suggestions/competitors?url={url}`

3. **Industry Categories**:
   - Marketing/SEO
   - E-commerce
   - Technology
   - Finance
   - Healthcare
   - Education
   - General

4. **Relevance Score**:
   - Industry match: 0.4
   - Keyword overlap: 0.4
   - Popularity: 0.2

5. **Cache**: 1 hour TTL for suggestions

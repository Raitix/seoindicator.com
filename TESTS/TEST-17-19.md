## Notes for Developer

1. **Service Location**: `backend/app/Services/IndustryClassifier.php`

2. **API Endpoints**:
   - `GET /api/v1/classify/industry?url={url}`
   - `GET /api/v1/config/industry/{industry}`

3. **Signal Detection**:
   - E-commerce: "cart", "checkout", "add to cart", "shop", "product"
   - SaaS: "pricing", "demo", "features", "free trial"
   - Local: address, phone, "hours", "near me", map embed
   - Blog: "blog", "article", "post", author bio
   - Agency: "services", "clients", "portfolio"

4. **Confidence Threshold**: 0.7 (70%) to classify, else 'other'

5. **Cache**: 24-hour TTL for classifications

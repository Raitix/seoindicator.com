## Notes for Developer

### Component Location
- **Integration Tests:** `frontend/__tests__/integration/`
- **Component:** `components/guides/SocialPreviewEmbed.tsx` (optional wrapper)

### Implementation Guidelines

1. **Reuse ToolEmbed** — SocialPreviewEmbed is a thin wrapper around ToolEmbed
2. **Guide slug → Tab mapping:**
   | guideSlug | defaultToolTab |
   |-----------|----------------|
   | `open-graph` | `facebook` |
   | `twitter-cards` | `twitter` |
   | other | `facebook` (default) |

3. **State key format:** `guide-{guideSlug}-social-preview`

4. **Error handling:** Use error boundary pattern from ToolEmbed

5. **Mobile-first CSS:** Design for mobile, enhance for larger screens

### ToolEmbed Props for Social Preview

```typescript
<ToolEmbed
  toolSlug="social-preview"
  defaultToolTab={guideSlug === 'twitter-cards' ? 'twitter' : 'facebook'}
  title={`Preview Your ${guideTitle}`}
  stateKey={`guide-${guideSlug}-social-preview`}
  showCta={true}
  ctaText="Use Full Social Media Preview"
/>
```

## Component Architecture

```
ReviewsPage (/locations/{id}/reviews)
├── ReviewStatsHeader
│   ├── AverageRatingDisplay (star + numeric)
│   ├── TotalReviewCount
│   ├── RatingDistributionBar (1-5 stars)
│   └── SentimentBreakdown (positive/neutral/negative)
├── ReviewsFilters
│   ├── RatingFilter (multi-select)
│   ├── SentimentFilter
│   ├── HasResponseFilter
│   └── DateRangePicker
├── SortDropdown
├── ReviewsList
│   └── ReviewCard (×N)
│       ├── AuthorInfo (name, avatar)
│       ├── StarRating
│       ├── ReviewText
│       ├── SentimentBadge
│       ├── OwnerResponse (if present)
│       └── ActionButtons (respond, view on Google)
└── ReviewResponseModal
    ├── OriginalReviewPreview
    ├── ResponseTextarea (max 4096 chars)
    ├── CharacterCounter
    └── Submit/Cancel buttons
```

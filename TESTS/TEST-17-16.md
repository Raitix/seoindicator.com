## Notes for Developer

1. **Service Location**: `backend/app/Services/TimeToFirstKeywordService.php`

2. **API Endpoint**: `GET /api/v1/admin/metrics/time-to-first-keyword`

3. **Bucket Thresholds**:
   - same_session: < 30 minutes
   - within_hour: 30 min - 60 min
   - within_day: 1 hour - 24 hours
   - within_week: 1 day - 7 days
   - never: no keyword

4. **Alert Threshold**: Median > 10 minutes triggers alert

5. **Integration**: Use `user_onboarding.completed_at` if available, otherwise `users.created_at`
